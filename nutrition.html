Why families choose Real

  • Good source of iron and calcium.
  • Helps control obesity and improves body metabolism as part of a balanced diet.
  • High dietary fibre for everyday digestion and lasting fullness.
  • Suitable for rotis, fulka, puri, paratha, biscuits, and cake.
  • No additives or preservatives — 100% atta, 0% maida.

Recommended roti tip

Mix 100 g atta with 70–75 ml water. Knead until soft, rest 15–20 minutes, then roll and cook on a hot tawa. Store in a cool, dry, hygienic place. Best before 3 months from packaging.
