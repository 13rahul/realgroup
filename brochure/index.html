Who we are

M/S. RealMulti Grain Mill Pvt. Ltd. manufactures the full Real brand range — Lokwan and Sehori Sharbati atta, rice atta, and whole wheat — milled with traditional chakki methods in a hygienic, ISO-certified facility at MIDC Baramati, India.

We deliver “gaon jaisa” taste with modern food-safety standards — built for Indian households and ready for international buyers who value purity, consistency, Halal compliance, and clean labelling.
