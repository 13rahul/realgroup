Who we are

M/S. RealMulti Grain Mill Pvt. Ltd. (Real Group India) is a trusted wheat and wheat atta exporter from India. We manufacture the full Real brand range — five SKUs of Lokwan and Sehori Sharbati atta, rice atta, and whole wheat — milled with traditional chakki methods in a hygienic mill at MIDC Baramati.

Every product is a Halal certified, APEDA registered quality product — ISO 9001:2015 and FSSAI licensed, with independent lab reports on our atta lines.
