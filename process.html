How we make it

From farm to soft roti

Traditional chakki grinding in a clean, hygienic mill � so every pack carries the taste of real freshness.

  1. Superior wheat crop

    We begin with carefully selected wheat crops known for quality grain and natural sweetness � the foundation of Premium MP Sharbati atta.

  2. Golden wheat grains

    Grains are cleaned and sorted so only golden, wholesome wheat enters the mill � purity you can taste in every roti.

  3. Grinding in a clean & hygienic chakki

    Stone-inspired chakki grinding in a controlled, hygienic environment preserves aroma, fibre, and the character of whole wheat.

  4. Absorbs more water

    Our atta takes up more water while kneading � giving you dough that feels soft, elastic, and easy to work with.

  5. Non-sticky dough

    Roll without struggle. The dough stays smooth and non-sticky for even, round chapatis every time.

  6. Soft, tasty & nutritious rotis

    The result: soft, fluffy, nutritious rotis that stay enjoyable through the meal � and invite one more phulka.
